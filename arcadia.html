Gaming Marketplace

Arcadia

Arcadia is a modern gaming marketplace designed to make discovering, buying, and managing digital games effortless. The goal was to create a platform that feels immersive like a game, yet functions with the clarity and trust of a professional marketplace.

Industry

Gaming

Scope

UI/UX, Prototyping, Wireframing, Design system

Challenge

Design a gaming marketplace that feels immersive and engaging while still being easy to navigate. The main challenge was balancing rich visuals, multiple product listings, and user actions without overwhelming the user or sacrificing clarity.

Solution

I created a visually driven marketplace experience that highlights games and digital assets through strong imagery and clear hierarchy.

The interface combines structured layouts with engaging visuals, making it easy for users to browse, explore, and purchase while staying immersed in the gaming context.

Competition

Many gaming marketplaces focus heavily on content but lack structure and usability, leading to cluttered experiences. This concept stands out by combining a modern UI style with a clear layout system that improves discoverability and keeps the experience both engaging and intuitive.

Outcomes

The result is a modern gaming marketplace concept that enhances product discovery and creates a more immersive browsing experience. It simplifies navigation while maintaining a strong visual identity that resonates with gaming audiences.
